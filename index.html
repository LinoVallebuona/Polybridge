Bridgewright

Span the gap with road, wood, steel and rope. Stay on budget. Then send the traffic across and see if physics agrees with you.

How to build

  • Drag from a joint to lay a beam, or click a joint, then click again to chain beams.
  • Road is the only thing vehicles drive on. Everything else holds the road up.
  • Rope and cable only pull. Wood and steel push and pull.
  • Double-click or right-click deletes. End a beam on another beam to attach it there.
  • Scroll or pinch to zoom; drag empty space to pan.
  • Finish under budget for 1 star, at 85% of the budget or less for 2, and at 70% or less for 3.
  • Space runs the test. Turn on Stress to see which beams are working hardest.
